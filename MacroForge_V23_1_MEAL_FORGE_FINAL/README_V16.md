# MacroForge V16

## Added
- Large Pakistani + global curated food expansion, including tandoori roti and many regional dishes.
- Broad global search with local-first matching plus Open Food Facts and USDA FoodData Central when online.
- Fixed Meal Coach logging by resolving every suggested meal component against the same Food Log library.
- Warning-only macro overage alert when protein, carbs, or fat exceed the corresponding goal by more than 10 g.
- Training Lab with less-manual workout generation, exercise science notes, progressive-overload guidance, and persistent exercise records.
- Exercise history can be kept indefinitely for month/year comparisons.

## Scientific basis
The UI links to primary/authoritative references for protein, resistance training progression, creatine, and hydration. Recipe nutrition remains reference data because homemade Pakistani food varies with recipe and oil.
