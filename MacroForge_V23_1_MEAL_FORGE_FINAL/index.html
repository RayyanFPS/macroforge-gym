PAKISTANI SCIENCE

Food timing examples

Before liftingRoti/rice + chicken, eggs + roti, or yogurt + fruit can provide familiar protein and carbohydrate. Keep the meal size and fat content comfortable for you.
After liftingUse a normal protein-containing meal such as chicken tikka + rice, daal + yogurt + rice, or qeema + roti. Total daily intake matters more than a tiny post-workout window.
Rest daysKeep protein consistent. Carbohydrate can be adjusted to activity and calorie needs rather than automatically eliminating it.

CREATINE SCIENCE

What the evidence supports

LOADINGOptional

A common research protocol uses about 0.3 g/kg/day for 5–7 days, often divided into four ~5 g doses. Loading is not required; smaller daily doses reach saturation more gradually.

ISSN / NIH ODS
MAINTENANCE3–5 g/day

After saturation, 3–5 g/day is a commonly studied maintenance range for creatine monohydrate.

ISSN / NIH ODS
FORMMonohydrate

Creatine monohydrate is the most extensively studied form. The tracker does not claim that flavored or alternative forms are superior.

Evidence-based reference
TIMINGConsistency first

There is no need to make creatine timing complicated. The tracker emphasizes whether the daily dose was taken rather than a special pre-workout window.

Practical adherence principle

Supplement nutrition values can differ by product and flavor. The Food Log uses generic references for creatine and protein powders; use the product label for exact serving nutrition.
